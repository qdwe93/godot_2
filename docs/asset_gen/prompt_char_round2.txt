The disc is a good bright cyan and the circle silhouette is correct. But there is a **dark grey/black
soft blob in the centre** of the disc, plus a soft radial gradient around it.

This matters more for the player than for anything else: the player is required to be **the
brightest object in the entire game**, and dark pixels in the middle drag its average brightness
down. Measured brightness came out at 0.85x of target because of this blob.

**No dark centre. No gradient. No soft shading. The disc is one flat cyan colour.**

only 22% of the sprite's slot has any solid pixels in it. The result is an unreadable smudge.

**This is the smallest sprite in the game.** It needs to be a compact, chunky, solid mass — think a
dart or an arrowhead, not an insect with legs. Thin protruding limbs are banned here.
